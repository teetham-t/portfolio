Engineering summary

Problem

A general chat model cannot reliably use the product’s 13 tools or answer from the site’s own equipment manuals.

What I built

A 792-example English/Thai training set; LoRA fine-tuning of a 30B model, finishing with a two-node run on two NVIDIA DGX Spark machines; hybrid retrieval over 41 manuals with correct citation pages; and a 289-case benchmark to measure all of it.

Jun 2025 – May 2026 · Meticuly internship

Production CT Classification Pipeline

DICOM → 2D views → BiomedCLIP + EVA-02 → Quality ranking

Picks the best post-craniectomy skull scan from all the CT series in a case, for the production CraNeXt system.

99.4% body-region accuracy · 96.1% craniectomy accuracy

  • DICOM
  • NIfTI
  • BiomedCLIP
  • EVA-02
Engineering summary

Problem

Each case has many CT series: different planes, slice thicknesses, and bone or soft-tissue windows. The first version ranked them only with rules on DICOM metadata and folder names (gantry tilt, missing slices, slice thickness, bone window, pixel spacing, plane). Metadata cannot show what is in the image, so the top pick could be a skull scan without the craniectomy.

What I built

Converted DICOM to NIfTI and made axial, coronal and sagittal 2D views. BiomedCLIP labels the body region (skull/brain, skull and neck, neck, chest, abdomen). For skull scans, a fine-tuned EVA-02 classifies pre- or post-craniectomy, and the ranking now prefers post-craniectomy scans. The output is a ranked JSON file.

Model choice

Benchmarked 8 models (5M–1,012M parameters) on accuracy, speed and size, and chose the smallest, EVA-02 Tiny.

Results

99.4% body-region accuracy (321 of 323 predictions) and 96.1% craniectomy accuracy (99 of 103).
